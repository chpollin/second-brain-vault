# Zettelkasten

A Zettelkasten is a collection of short linked notes, best known from the sociologist Niklas Luhmann ([[Luhmann 1981 - Kommunikation mit Zettelkästen]]). A vault adopts its idea of small linked notes and a few entry points, see [[Map of Content]].
