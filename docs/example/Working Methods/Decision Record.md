# Decision Record

A decision record keeps a choice together with its grounds, the alternative considered and the conditions under which it holds. Months later, the reasons can still be found and checked.

Reusing a decision elsewhere starts with its conditions. Example: [[Decision Image Master Format]]. It follows [[One Statement One Place]].
