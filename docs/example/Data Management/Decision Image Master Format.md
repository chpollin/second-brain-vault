# Decision Image Master Format

## Decision

Archival masters of photographs are kept as uncompressed TIFF. Access copies are JPEG.

## Grounds

- An [[Archival Master]] keeps the full information of the scan, and an [[Access Copy]] can be made from it at any time.
- TIFF is openly documented and widely read, see [[File Format]].
- The scanner delivers uncompressed TIFF without loss, see [[Scan Protocol 2025]].

## Alternative considered

Keeping only high-quality JPEG files was rejected, because each re-save loses information.

## Conditions

The decision covers still images from the photo archive. Audio, video and text need their own decision.
