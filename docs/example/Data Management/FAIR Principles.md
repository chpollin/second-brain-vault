# FAIR Principles

The FAIR principles ask that research data be findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable ([[Wilkinson 2016 - The FAIR Guiding Principles]]).

They are served by a [[Metadata Standard]], a [[Persistent Identifier]] and a clear [[Licence]].
