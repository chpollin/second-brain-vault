# Online Presentation Photo Archive

The public online presentation of the photo archive, built from the project's knowledge and its [[Access Copy|access copies]].

## Findings from testing

- The first public test showed that many photographs carry no rights statement, so visitors cannot tell what reuse is allowed.
- The finding went back into the project: rights became a required field, see [[Metadata Standard]] and [[Licence]].
