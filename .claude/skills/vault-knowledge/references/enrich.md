# enrich

A named existing document brought to one coherent current version with external sources, evidenced corrections and explicitly open conflicts.

## Steps

1. Read the existing state, meaning the target document in full, its Related links and its MOC. A statement a neighbour already carries stays there and appears in the target as a link.
2. Gather the material. When the operator supplies the finding and its source and declares the source as read, take it as opened and skip the research. Otherwise research external sources by web search and fetch, scholarly sources preferred, DOI or URL carried along. The source rules of SKILL.md apply.
3. Read the old holdings against the new findings. For every older section decide whether it stays unchanged, is corrected with evidence, is merged into the new version, or is dropped because a neighbour carries it canonically. The decision per section belongs in the output.
4. Work new and corrected statements into the existing sections. Every statement carries its reference, a scholarly one as `(Author, Year)` plus a full entry under `## Sources`, a web source as a footnote with access date. Where a document mixes research finding, practice consensus and own observation, the apparatus names which of the three applies at the evidence. Keep the minimum source count of the vault convention. No volatile quantities.
5. Walk the inbound links and decide per location, as SKILL.md prescribes.
6. Carry placement along, linking new concepts at first occurrence, updating the Synthesis section where the new sources change the connections, and changing tags only within the controlled vocabulary.
7. Verify that valid statements, concrete examples and their sources survived, that the corrections are covered by the evidence named, and that the document is now one version rather than old holdings with sections appended. On a substantial extension, recommend the check mode on the document.

## Output

What was added or corrected and why, the decision per older section, the decision per location in the neighbours, new sources with reference, unverified sources with reason, open conflicts, and overlaps moved elsewhere.
