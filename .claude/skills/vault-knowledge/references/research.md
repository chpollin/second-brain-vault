# research

A subject question researched and the evidenced knowledge integrated into the vault, new documents included. The result is findable, source-bound vault knowledge.

## Steps

1. Determine the gap. Search over titles, aliases and `query-topics`, then read the relevant notes and their MOC. Establish what the holdings already answer and which concrete question stays open, and set the research depth to that gap.
2. Read primary sources and record locations. Scholarly work and official technical documentation carry different evidential functions, and a framework guide evidences no general productivity claim. The source rules of SKILL.md apply. End the research when the agreed question is sufficiently evidenced or the remaining gap can be named concretely.
3. Integrate into existing notes by the enrich mode.
4. A new document needs a term or a use its holdings do not yet cover. Choose its document class and apply that convention, and take source count and frontmatter from the document rules. A thin source base justifies no invented evidence, so deliver the evidenced part and name the integration that is not finished.
5. Place statements where they belong, link their sources, and attach new notes through the fitting MOC or a reachable neighbour. Avoid doubled explanations between general architecture knowledge and project knowledge. Merging or deleting existing documents follows the refactor mode and needs its own order.
6. Produce no separate research report when the integrated notes already carry the result.

## Output

The integrated findings with their target notes, the checks actually run, and the remaining uncertainty. A question about a repository does not order a change to its code.
