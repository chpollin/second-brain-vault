# Project

Second Brain brings together knowledge held by a person, knowledge about a person or knowledge within a domain. It supports the organization, connection, examination and further use of that knowledge. Modelling can make distinctions and relationships explicit where the material and the task require it. The function is independent of a particular domain or output: a research question, argument, text, decision, model or tool may result from working with the knowledge.

The operator's personal research environment is one application of this general function. In that application, a maintained vault holds developed knowledge, source readings, methodological reflections and cross-project relations. Project repositories retain the knowledge governing their own material and implementation. Available agents use selected parts of this environment under the actual host's permissions. A collection about a person is distinct from the optional Research Persona, which specifies the user's research profile and working agreement.

The existing research question asks under what conditions earlier reasoning can be recovered, transferred with justification, examined through research artefacts and revised for later use. The [manuscript](paper.md) and [evaluation procedure](evaluation.md) investigate this particular application. They do not define the only permitted use of Second Brain or establish a general benefit across persons and domains.

## Repository purpose

This repository supplies a reusable vault template with synthetic examples. Its readers know Obsidian and have worked with a coding agent. They can adopt its rules, conventions and procedures with their own knowledge. The template abstracts a maintained personal research vault while excluding the source owner's research corpus, personal profile, memory, voice-specific writing modes and business procedures.

The [specification](specification.md) defines the implemented template. The [integration contract](integration.md) distinguishes this public contribution from the private working environment and its separate tools. The [method page](design.md) explains the approach to readers without prior knowledge, with a graph of the template and a synthetic example domain. The [knowledge explorer](design.md#knowledge-explorer) supports reading those notes in their linked context and inspecting the passages behind each reference. The private working environment retains its own dialogue and operational tools.

## Method context

The [Promptotyping method](https://github.com/DigitalHumanitiesCraft/Promptotyping) supplies the relation between maintained project knowledge and the development of research artefacts. The template applies this through its `project-knowledge` skill and knowledge-document convention. [Grounded Vault](https://github.com/DigitalHumanitiesCraft/grounded-vault) provides a related architecture for source-grounded claims. [Research Mission Control](https://github.com/DigitalHumanitiesCraft/research-mission-control) addresses the coordination of research work. The paper integrates knowledge organization, source review and research-artefact development through Knowledge Structures' distinction among knowledge kinds, organizational patterns and collection purposes. It connects artefact findings returning to project knowledge with the later reuse of that knowledge. The Knowledge Structures manuscript is a conceptual simulation and supplies no empirical cases. Promptotyping retains its separate ZfdG paper project. Grounded Vault and Research Mission Control retain their own methodological contributions. These methods retain their own specifications and are not installed by cloning this template.

## Structure develops through use

Start with sources, notes, meaningful links and overview documents. Add a relation vocabulary, argument note or more explicit model when an actual task reveals a distinction or connection that the existing arrangement cannot express adequately. A proposed structure remains optional until its purpose and maintenance needs have been examined in use. The [research workflow](research-workflow.md) carries the corresponding working distinctions.

## Evidence boundary

The [skill evaluation](skill-evaluation.md) records bounded synthetic trials, including a separate persona initialisation trial. Such trials establish only the behaviour recorded for their cases. They establish neither independent scholarly validation nor a general benefit over work without the skills. A successful structural check, a prepared context and an accepted research result are different forms of evidence.
