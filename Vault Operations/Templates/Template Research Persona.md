# Template Research Persona

The skill `persona-init` adapts this structure in the owner's language. This is an empty form, with no personal claims. By default the resulting file lives at `.local/persona/Research Persona.md`, outside Git and the generated explorer. Do not fill this distributable template with the owner's information.

Use valid frontmatter from [[Convention Frontmatter Field Profiles]], with `type: knowledge`, `status: draft`, the actual creation date and registered tags. Set the copied title to `Research Persona`. Document maturity and the owner's confirmation of individual claims are separate.

## Profile

Write connected prose that the owner can read or listen to as a whole. Describe their field, materials, research purpose and practical work. Where supported, connect foundational research, such as a dissertation, to their understanding of research processes, data modelling, workflows and tools. Name an original contribution precisely and distinguish it from ideas attributed to earlier literature.

Keep questions, evidence labels and instructions to the agent outside this narrative. An initial version can be short. Omit unsupported details and keep unresolved claims below.

## Working agreement

Keep only the owner's applicable preferences and the minimum rules needed to use the profile. Separate preferences supplied by the owner from proposed collaboration behaviour. The profile does not grant tool access or override vault rules.

### Co-researcher

Work with the owner on the stated task. Give a reasoned assessment, retrieve relevant sources selectively and distinguish your conclusions from the owner's recorded views. First-person statements by the agent express the agent's assessment.

### Interview draft

Use the owner's first-person perspective only when requested. Label the answer set as a simulation for review. Give readable answers followed by a compact account of supporting sources, inferences and missing personal information. Never invent an experience or treat a simulated answer as a confirmed preference.

## Evidence and confirmation

| Claim | Source and location | Source kind | Owner confirmation and scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| A specific statement included in the profile | Note and section, publication passage, or dated statement supplied in the conversation | Self-report, source-backed fact, inference or simulation | Confirmed with date and scope, or pending |

Record only task-relevant excerpts needed to explain a claim. A publication can support a research contribution without establishing a current personal preference. Silence, storage and model agreement are not confirmation. A local request such as shortening one answer does not automatically change a general preference.

## Open points

Keep missing information and unresolved interpretations here, with the smallest question that would resolve each one. Remove a question once the answer has been integrated. Do not present unknowns as defects in the person.

## Sources

List the actual sources read and the dates of relevant personal statements. Link technical or method definitions to their own notes instead of copying them into the profile.
