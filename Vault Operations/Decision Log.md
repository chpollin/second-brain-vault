# Decision Log

The history and the reasons of the rules, newest first. The rules themselves stand in [[CLAUDE]], in `.claude/rules/` and in the conventions. The decisions in force are registered in [[VAULT-OPERATIONS#Decisions in force]].

## 2026-09-20 Research context across repositories

The operator approved Second Brain as a personal research environment for working with AI
agents. Its knowledge remains distributed between the personal vault and project repositories.
[[Repo Directory]] identifies the maintained locations, and
[[Convention Knowledge Documents#Bridge to the vault]] governs how selected project context
is read and related to a new task. Skills execute bounded procedures over that knowledge.

The task determines which sources are relevant. A source from another project retains its
original scope, and an inaccessible location remains an explicit gap. Links neither install
tools nor authorize changes. HOME exposes the repository route, the relevant skills and their
evaluation cases follow the convention, and the repository knowledge describes the distinction
between the reusable template, the separate exporter and the separate work interface.

The network consists of maintained documents and explicit references. Automatic retrieval
across repositories and a combined live agent interface remain possible extensions. This
revision claims neither their implementation nor an observed improvement in research work.

## 2026-09-19 Personal research persona initialisation

The operator commissioned a reusable procedure for constructing a personal research persona.
The template now supplies `persona-init` and [[Template Research Persona]], with separate
profile prose, working agreement, provenance and open points. Questions carry enough context
to be answered without opening unseen documents. Explicit personal statements retain their
scope, while simulations and inferences do not become confirmed facts through storage.

The default personal output is `.local/persona/Research Persona.md`. Existing Git and explorer
exclusions cover this location. The session-start rule discovers the profile for relevant
personal collaboration, while interview simulation requires an explicit request. The change
adds no model runtime and publishes no profile. Bounded behavioural checks and their limits
are recorded in `knowledge/skill-evaluation.md`.

## 2026-09-19 Skill evaluation and bounded execution

Synthetic trials exposed an ambiguous external read boundary, conflicting synthesis defaults, repeated confirmation after an explicit update order and an ungrammatical English example. The affected skills now state the intended behaviour directly. Authorized content repairs refresh the generated explorer before their closing checks when it is present. Read-only runs preserve the files.

Every skill now carries reusable evaluation cases. Expected outcomes remain separate from observed results in the template's `knowledge/skill-evaluation.md`. The dissolution cases preserve registered fixtures, require explicit evidence that a logistical question is resolved and leave fictional bibliographic disagreements unsettled without a source. The source fixtures and the checks that expect their defects stay unchanged.

## 2026-09-19 Pointers need a target

When skills were rewritten so that they reference rule values instead of copying them, several rules turned out to stand only in the old skill and not in the document the new pointer named. They would have been lost silently. A pointer therefore replaces a copy only after the rule was found at the target, and the loss audit of a skill change lists every such pointer with its target.

## 2026-09-19 Propagation after change

A review of one knowledge folder showed that duplicated text was the smaller problem. Concept notes had been revised while the documents that restate them kept the earlier state in the present tense, most often old collecting documents. No procedure inspected the neighbours after a change. The rule now stands in `.claude/rules/documents.md` so that it applies to every change, and the maintenance skill carries the step of walking the inbound links.
